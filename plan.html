The call

Run a 90-day pilot with one council. Residents report dumping in three taps, the council acts from an email, and our crews collect it the next working day and recycle it. Every council that signs becomes a collection contract feeding our recycling.

Why councils will pay

  • They already pay for it. Greater Geelong had 6,619 requests to remove dumped rubbish in 2024‑25, and more than 3,000 by January this financial year. City of Greater Geelong
  • Disposal alone runs to hundreds of thousands. Geelong's mayor put this year's disposal bill at around $300,000 by February, before labour, plant and transport. Ocean Grove Voice
  • The state is funding clean-ups. $21.5 million announced in October 2025, including $8.5 million to help councils and land managers with clean-up costs. Premier of Victoria
  • Nobody knows who to call. Council, EPA Victoria, VicTrack, state roads and Parks Victoria each own part of the problem. We route it automatically and copy EPA by waste type. EPA Victoria

Why we win

  • Nobody else closes the loop. Snap Send Solve emails councils for free and sells them integrations and analytics. Nobody collects it, recycles it and proves it. Snap Send Solve
  • The clock is the hook. Residents watch it move, and fast councils get credit instead of complaints.
  • We know prevention works. Making ten things free to drop off cut roadside dumping, and we can offer free drop-off days to pilot councils. Need the before and after numbers
  • It scales past our crews. Rubbish.com.au's registered collectors can cover areas our crews don't. Proposed

What could go wrong

RiskHow we handle it
Councils see a conflict: the reporting tool belongs to the collector.Routing is neutral and the council picks who collects. Reports by email are free whoever collects.
Councils don't want a public clock.It runs against their own targets, and every collection ends on good news. Start with a council that wants the credit.
Scrutiny turns into naming people.Scrutiny is on the dumping, not people. Regos go only to the council and EPA. Legal advice before launch.
Fake reports, hazardous waste, faces in photos.Duplicates merged, photo and pin before a crew goes, hazards flagged to EPA and never handled by a general crew, faces and plates blurred.

The pilot

  • Greater Geelong has the volume, and our crews and drop-off are local.
  • Hepburn Shire is smaller and already a client through the Clunes collection.
  • Hobsons Bay knows us from the collection booking tender.

Measure: median time to collect, share collected by the next working day, m³ kept out of landfill, kilograms recovered, and resident follow-up calls.
